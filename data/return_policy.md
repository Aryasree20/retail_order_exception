# Retail Returns and Order Exceptions Policy (fictional, version 1)

## Clause P1 — Standard returns
Standard returns may be requested within **30 calendar days after confirmed delivery**, inclusive of the day-30 boundary. Outside the window, return is not normally eligible unless a separately approved exception applies.

## Clause P2 — Damaged items
Documented damaged-item claims inside the standard window may be considered for replacement. If no stock is available, send to human review for an alternative resolution. Evidence and approval are required.

## Clause P3 — Delivered but not received
A delivery status of DELIVERED does not by itself resolve a customer dispute. Route to delivery investigation; do not auto-refund.

## Clause P4 — Cancelled but charged
If an order is CANCELLED and payment is CAPTURED, request payment verification and refund review. Do not assert that the refund was executed.

## Clause P5 — Money movement
No automated financial transactions are authorized by this assignment. Any refund or replacement is a recommendation for human approval.

## Clause P6 — Missing/conflicting evidence
If order identity, policy text or evidence is absent or contradictory, choose REQUEST_INFO or MANUAL_REVIEW rather than inventing facts.
